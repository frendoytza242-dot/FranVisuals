# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (greenfield choice, confirmed by user — recommended for a mostly-static portfolio site).

## Users

Primary users are two audiences evaluating FranVisuals as a graphic designer:
- Potential clients looking to hire a graphic designer for projects.
- Recruiters evaluating work for a design position.

Their job on the site: review the portfolio of graphic design projects and decide whether to reach out.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio website for FranVisuals, a graphic designer. It exists to showcase design projects and give visitors a direct way to get in touch. Success means a visitor viewing the work and being able to reach out via WhatsApp with minimal friction.

## Positioning

A personal design portfolio built around FranVisuals' own project work and visual identity — not a generic template or agency site.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse the portfolio (likely first on mobile, given WhatsApp is the contact channel) to review projects, then tap through to a WhatsApp conversation to make contact.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Showcase graphic design projects (case studies / project gallery) — over 1000 completed projects, so the gallery must scale via curation/filtering, not a literal 1000-item list.
- Categories: Streamers, Deportes (Sports), Empresa (Corporate) — generalist, adapts to most briefs; treat as the primary filter/tag set, open to more.
- Contact mechanism: a WhatsApp deep link pre-filled with the message "¡Buenas Fran, estoy interesado en que armemos un proyecto juntos! 🙌" to +54 9 11 3888-0492.
- Social links: Twitter/X (https://x.com/TronkoGFX) and Instagram (https://www.instagram.com/frann.visuals/).
- Undecided: whether case studies get individual detail pages or a single scrolling showcase.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: FranVisuals.
- Social presence: X @TronkoGFX, Instagram @frann.visuals — both should be linked from the site.
- Visual reference volunteered by the user: https://wodes.com.ar/casos/ (binding stylistic reference for the portfolio/case-study experience; to be evaluated and translated into FranVisuals' own visual world during design, not copied verbatim).

## Evidence on Hand

Project imagery source: user-provided Google Drive folder (https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1f-8Hv3godNuowOtPEaLWfoT9GHbfJ1SD). No images have been downloaded into the repository yet. Do not fabricate placeholder projects, client names, testimonials, or results beyond what the Drive folder and user confirm; use clearly-marked placeholders only until real assets are pulled in.

## Product Principles

1. The work is the hero — layout and design system should foreground project imagery over decoration.
2. Contact must be effortless: the WhatsApp path should be reachable from anywhere on the site in one tap.
3. Built for a graphic designer's audience — the site's own craft is part of the pitch, since visitors are evaluating design judgment.
4. Stay honest: no fabricated clients, testimonials, or project results before real content exists.
